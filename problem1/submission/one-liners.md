# MetaboQuest: three one-liners (Problem Statement 1)

- **Trigger:** "GLP-1: how much is fact, and how much is fear?" plus "What makes you unsure about GLP-1?", so a sceptical scroller taps the doubt they already have (safety, side effects, muscle, weight regain, diabetes, "is it for me?").
- **Value:** Three questions on that doubt, each with instant myth-vs-fact feedback and a source link, then a plain-language topic takeaway before the knowledge score. No login, no promises, no drug names.
- **Next step:** "See if the programme fits you" / "Talk to a Steer doctor" opens Steer's site after a four-step view of diagnostics, doctor consult, continuous care and habits that last; nothing from the quiz is sent to Steer.
